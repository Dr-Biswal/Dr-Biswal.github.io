About me

I'm an engineer and educator working at the intersection of electrical power, the Internet of Things, and artificial intelligence. My journey started in electrical and electronics engineering, deepened through a master's in power electronics, and matured into a Ph.D. where I built machine-learning-driven demand-side management for smart greenhouses — funded research that cut energy costs by nearly 43% in a real PV-powered deployment.

Today I teach IoT, AI, and machine learning at SIT College of Engineering, Kolhapur, while pursuing an Executive M.Tech in AI & Data Science at IIT Patna. What drives me most is turning research into systems that actually work in the field — and mentoring engineers who can build them.
